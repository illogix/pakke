# Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Pakke is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and the web. It does not collect,
store, or share any personal data.

## What stays on your device

Your puzzle library, progress, daily puzzle results and settings are stored
only on your device, inside the app's own storage (or your browser's storage
for the web version). They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or
clearing the site's data in your browser, removes them.

## No network, accounts or tracking

Pakke has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
third-party SDKs. The iPhone and iPad app never connects to the internet. The
web version only downloads the game itself, from the site that serves it.

## Files you choose to export

Backups and exported puzzles are files you create yourself, through the
system's share sheet or a download. Pakke does not see where you send or save
them. A backup contains your library, progress and settings, and nothing else.

## Children

Pakke collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new
effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at
